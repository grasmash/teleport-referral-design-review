Decision 1: friends who join monthly

A friend who joins monthly can only get $29.99 off, because that's the whole first month. The card promises $50. Which should we do?

  1. Reword the card: "$50 off a yearly membership, or their first month."
  2. Offer the friend discount on yearly memberships only.
  3. Give monthly friends the full $50, spread over their first two bills.

Decision 2: lifetime members' credit

Lifetime members have nothing that renews, so their $50 can't come off a bill. It now goes on their account as a balance, and their card says "You have $50 in referral credit on your account." Today there's nothing to spend it on. What should it pay for?

  1. Store purchases: pedals and accessories.
  2. A gift membership for a friend.
  3. Something else.
