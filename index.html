Our own referral links: design review

Draft screens from a test site, not live. Terms: a friend who joins with a member's link gets $50 off; the member gets $50 back on their next renewal, for every friend, no limit. Each member shares one short link ending in /r/ plus a code.

Open question: a friend joining monthly can only get $29.99 off (one month). Should the card say "$50 off a yearly membership, or their first month", should the discount be yearly only, or should monthly friends get $50 spread over two bills?
